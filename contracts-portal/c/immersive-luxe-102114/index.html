01Parties and scope

This agreement is between Meridian AI Solutions ("Meridian"), represented by Zion Farquharson, Founder and CEO, and Immersive Luxe Events, represented by ("Client"), and covers the build and operation of a website, voice concierge and lead automation for the Client's events business.

Meridian will deliver the following (the "Services"):

  • A multi-page website in the Client's brand
  • An inquiry form with email alerts to the Client's team
  • A voice concierge tuned to the Client's brand and services
  • Lead qualification by event package, with a separate follow-up path for each package
  • A private concierge page for high-ticket inquiries
  • A team area with secure login and a shared lead list
  • A call summary emailed to the Client's team after each voice conversation
  • A payment link shown after an inquiry, set to the down-payment percentage of the package the guest chose

WhatsApp alerts and calendar booking are not included. If the Client wants them, Meridian will quote them separately in writing.

02Fees and payment

The Client pays a $5,000 integration fee in five monthly payments of $1,000, plus a $300 monthly service fee that starts when the website goes live.

03Monthly service, term and usage

The $300 monthly fee covers hosting, the tools that run the website and automation, the voice platform up to the included minutes below, and up to 2 hours of small edits each month.

Term. The initial term is 12 months from the launch date. After that it continues month to month, and either party may end it with 30 days' written notice.

Included voice minutes. The monthly fee includes 300 voice minutes. Minutes are the total length of voice conversations handled by the concierge, as reported by the voice platform. Unused minutes do not carry over to the next month.

Extra minutes. Minutes above 300 in a month cost $0.35 per minute and are added to the next monthly invoice. Meridian will email the Client a usage report each month and will tell the Client when usage reaches 240 minutes, which is 80 percent of the included amount.

04Client responsibilities and changes

The Client supplies its brand name, logo, photos and list of services within 7 days of the first payment. Delays on the Client's side move the launch date by the same number of days.

  • The Client reviews and approves the website and the concierge's script before launch.
  • The Client tells Meridian what the concierge should and should not say, for example that it never quotes prices.
  • The Client is responsible for telling callers that the concierge is automated and that conversations may be recorded, where the law requires it.
  • Anything outside section 1, such as extra pages, new integrations or new features, is quoted and agreed in writing before work starts, and is billed as an extra.

The Client supplies its package names, package prices and the down-payment percentage for each package, and sets up the payment link with its own payment provider. Any fees charged by the payment provider are the Client's.

05Ownership, confidentiality and termination

Ownership. The Client owns its own content, including its brand, logo, photos and the lead data collected through the site, and may ask for a copy of its lead data at any time. Meridian keeps ownership of its underlying tools, templates and automation workflows, and the Client may use the delivered website and concierge for as long as it keeps up its payments under this agreement.

Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, including the Client's lead data, and uses it only to carry out this agreement.

Late payment. If a payment is more than 7 days late, Meridian may pause work or the service after written notice, until the payment is made.

Termination. Either party may end this agreement if the other commits a serious breach and does not fix it within 14 days of written notice. If the Client ends the agreement during the initial 12-month term for any other reason, the unpaid integration fee payments and the monthly fees for the rest of the initial term remain payable.

No guarantees. Meridian does not guarantee a number of leads or bookings. The voice concierge is automated and may sometimes make mistakes. Meridian's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Client paid in the 3 months before a claim.

Governing law. The laws of The Bahamas apply to this agreement.
