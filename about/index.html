ABOUT

A small team with a direct line to every client.

Meridian AI Solutions builds voice agents and websites for businesses in the Bahamas. Two founders, working hands-on with every project.

WHERE THE NAME COMES FROM

Connecting businesses to their customers.

A meridian is the line that runs from pole to pole, part of the shared grid that makes navigation, trade and time possible.

We chose the name for what we want to do for local businesses: make sure the people trying to reach them can, whether they call, search or send a message.

HOW WE WORK

Composed
