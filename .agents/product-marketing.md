## Product Overview

- **Company:** Meridian AI Solutions, an AI integration company in Nassau, The Bahamas.
- **What we sell (done-for-you builds, not self-serve software):**
  1. AI voice agents: answer routine calls, collect details, route to staff, optional booking.
  2. Messenger bots: answer common Facebook Messenger questions and capture enquiries.
  3. Email bots: draft or send routine replies and route enquiries.
  4. Websites and portals: mobile-first sites with clear call, book and enquire paths, plus staff or customer portals.
  5. Workflow automation: turn forms and enquiries into assigned tasks, reminders and next steps.
- **Tagline:** "Your Money$ on the Line"
- **Pricing:** TODO (setup fee and monthly? per project?)
- **Category:** Agencies and B2B services, plus local services. Load both industry packs in `reverse-engineer-marketing`.

## Target Audience

- Small and mid-sized businesses in Nassau and across The Bahamas that handle many phone, Messenger and email enquiries.
- Verticals in the public demo: restaurants, dental practices, legal practices, retail stores and auto parts. Proven so far: education (a private school).
- Buyer: the owner or practice manager. Often the same person who answers the phone when staff are busy.

## Problems & Pain Points

- Missed calls and slow replies mean lost bookings and sales ("your money's on the line").
- Staff spend hours on the same questions: hours, prices, availability.
- Enquiries arrive by phone, Messenger, email and walk-ins, with no single place to follow up.
- Websites that are outdated, hard to use on phones, or have no clear way to enquire.

## Differentiation

- Local: based in Nassau, built for Bahamian businesses and callers.
- Hands-on: clients work directly with the two founders who plan and build the system.
- Practical: built around the client's real enquiries and the tools they already use.
- Voice: agents aim to sound "like a good receptionist: calm, clear and unhurried."
- One partner for website, phone, messaging and follow-up workflow, instead of several vendors.

## Objections

- "AI will sound robotic or annoy my customers." Answer: voice and language are tested for the audience, and calls hand off to a person.
- "It's too expensive or too technical for a small business." TODO: pricing answer.
