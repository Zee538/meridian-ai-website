PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information.

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This policy covers this website, meridian-aisolutions.com.

What we collect

If you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, business, email address, phone number (optional), your interest and your message. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to discuss work you've asked about. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it

This site is hosted by Netlify, which also processes contact form submissions and keeps standard server logs. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads. We don't use advertising trackers on this website.

Website analytics

We run our own simple analytics to see which pages are visited. For each page view we record the page, the website you came from (domain only), your country, and whether your screen is a phone, tablet or desktop. We don't use cookies or store anything on your device for this. To count visitors without identifying anyone, we turn your IP address and browser details into a short code that changes every day, and we never store the IP address itself. We keep these records for up to 14 months. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we don't record your visit.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to follow up on the conversation, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by emailing info@meridian-aisolutions.com.

Agents and bots we build for clients

This policy does not cover calls, messages or emails handled by an AI voice agent or bot that we build for a client. How those are handled is set out in our arrangement with that client.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@meridian-aisolutions.com.
