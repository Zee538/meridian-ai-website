PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information.

Last updated: November 17, 2026. This policy covers our messaging and phone services and this website, meridian-aisolutions.com.

Meridian AI Solutions ("Meridian", "we", "us") is a Bahamian-owned company based in Nassau, The Bahamas. We build AI assistants and automation for businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Messages and calls. The content of messages you send us on WhatsApp or other channels, and details you share if you call our business line.
  • Contact details. Your name, phone number, WhatsApp profile name and email address, if you provide them.
  • Business details. Information you tell us about your business, such as its name, industry, needs and timeline, so we can understand how to help.
  • Scheduling details. Times and notes for meetings or demos you ask us to arrange.

We do not ask for payment card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers in chat. Please do not send them.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To understand your needs and prepare a relevant proposal or demo.
  • To schedule and confirm meetings.
  • To keep a record of our conversations so we can follow up.
  • To improve our services and keep them secure.

3. Use of AI

Some of our messaging and phone services are answered by an AI assistant that works on behalf of our team. The assistant tells you when it is an AI. Our team may read conversations and take over at any time. Meetings and demos are only confirmed after a member of our team approves them.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our services, and only as needed. These include messaging platform providers, cloud productivity and storage providers, workflow automation providers, voice and AI service providers. They process information on our behalf and are expected to protect it. We may also share information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep information

We keep conversation and contact records for as long as they are useful for the relationship or for our legitimate business purposes, then delete or anonymize them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see section 7).

6. Security

We use access controls and reputable providers to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to stop messaging you at any time. Reply "STOP" or email us.

To make a request, email Zion@meridian-aisolutions.com from the contact details you used with us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at people under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Additional information for visitors to this website

The sections above apply to our messaging and phone services. This section covers meridian-aisolutions.com.

What we collect on this website

If you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, business, email address, phone number (optional), your interest and your message. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share. We use your details only to reply to you and to discuss work you've asked about. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes website information

This site is hosted by Netlify, which also processes contact form submissions and keeps standard server logs. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads. We don't use advertising trackers on this website.

Website analytics

We run our own simple analytics to see which pages are visited. For each page view we record the page, the website you came from (domain only), your country, and whether your screen is a phone, tablet or desktop. We don't use cookies or store anything on your device for this. To count visitors without identifying anyone, we turn your IP address and browser details into a short code that changes every day, and we never store the IP address itself. We keep these records for up to 14 months. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we don't record your visit.

Agents and bots we build for clients

This policy does not cover calls, messages or emails handled by an AI voice agent or bot that we build for one of our clients. How those are handled is set out in our arrangement with that client.
