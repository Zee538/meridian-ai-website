QUESTIONS

Before you get in touch.

Can you connect the tools we already use?

Often, yes. We check your forms, calendars, CRM and other tools before confirming the scope. Available integrations, permissions and how your team works determine what can be connected.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. Each service works on its own, and you can add another whenever it makes sense.

Which languages does the voice agent speak?

We choose the voice and language for your audience, then test representative calls before launch. Ask us about the languages you need.

What happens when a caller asks for something the agent can't handle?

We agree that with you in advance. Typically the agent takes the caller's details and a short note, so your team can follow up.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on the systems involved. After a first conversation we'll give you a clear schedule in writing.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the work involved, integrations and expected usage. We agree the scope and quote before work begins.

Do you only work with businesses in Nassau?

We're based in the Bahamas and focus on businesses here, though we're happy to talk if you're elsewhere in the country.
